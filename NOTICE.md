checks, finite native-log message budget and related regressions are adapted from
[dd2673/dsh-cross-session-agent](https://github.com/dd2673/dsh-cross-session-agent)
and its [lilongsky compatibility changes](https://github.com/lilongsky/dsh-cross-session-agent/tree/61d04728f3d5b1a0947ab4a1259a35c6aed9a605),
both distributed under MIT. That project in turn derives from this original
project. This contribution preserves the original three-tool messaging scope,
native Session references, locale behavior and delivery-card preferences.
